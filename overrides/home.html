About

Urban Physics & Computational Fluid Dynamics

Cities are hot, turbulent, and complex — and I use CFD to make sense of them. My research maps the invisible forces shaping urban climate: wind threading between buildings, the cooling shadow of a tree canopy, heat trapped in a street canyon. Through high-fidelity simulations and data-driven analysis, I build physics-informed tools that help design cities people can actually live in.
